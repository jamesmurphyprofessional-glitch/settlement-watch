HOW THIS PACKAGE WAS (AND WAS NOT) DELIVERED

This archive was built on the author’s machine. It was NOT emailed,
uploaded, or otherwise transmitted to the Internal Revenue Service by
the software that generated it.

The IRS does not accept unsolicited programs through public email.
Sending a zip file to a general IRS address is the wrong channel and
will usually be blocked.

If James Murphy (or anyone else) wants the Service to see this tool,
use an official path — do not improvise:

  1. Software / analytics interest
     IRS procurement and official technology intake. github.com/IRSgov
     is not a drop-box for investigative tools.

  2. A specific taxpayer you believe under-reported income
     That is a tip, not a software submission:
       Form 3949-A (Information Referral)
       Form 211 (Whistleblower, if you are claiming an award)
     https://www.irs.gov/

  3. Do not
     - Email random irs.gov inboxes with this zip
     - Call it an official IRS product
     - Treat demo names as real targets
     - File a SAR from this tool (you are not the BSA filer)

The cover note (COVER-NOTE.txt) is a letter you can print or attach IF
you choose a legitimate channel. It is not a stamp that the IRS asked
for this, reviewed this, or will use this.
